Movie "Gezhi Town" premiered on December 6, 2025. It was then released in Australia and New Zealand on December 18, and in the United States and Canada on December 25. The movie is based on Lan Xiaolong's original story 'The Battle of Yichang,' focusing on the key battles of the defense of Shipai during the 1940-1943 campaign in western Hubei.
